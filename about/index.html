About

About me

I'm a software engineer with 7+ years of experience building games, VR/AR applications, multiplayer systems, and backend services, mostly in Unity and C#. I've shipped across Meta Quest, PlayStation VR2, Steam, HTC Vive, Pico, iOS, Android, HoloLens, and enterprise VR environments.

My career started in XR (spatial computing, mixed reality, therapeutic VR, AR product experiences) and then expanded into large-scale multiplayer and live-service engineering through six years on Gorilla Tag, one of Meta Quest's largest titles: 10M+ players, $100M+ in lifetime revenue, and repeated #1 placements on the Quest charts. Systems I built there on Redis, Cosmos DB, and Azure Functions ran in production at that scale, alongside gameplay work.

I studied Computer Science (Games) at the University of Southern California.
